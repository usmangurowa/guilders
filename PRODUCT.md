# PRODUCT.md — Guilders Limited

## What this is
Corporate website for Guilders Limited (RC 9819868), a private Nigerian company
limited by shares, incorporated 1 September 2026 in Makurdi, Benue State.
One company operating five arms under one standard: technology & digital
solutions; transport & logistics (fleet, haulage, dispatch); general trade,
contracts & supply; asset ownership & leasing; ventures & investments.

## Unique mechanism
A single disciplined Nigerian company that owns productive assets and runs
diversified operating lines itself — it doesn't broker, it owns and operates.
Named for the guilder (the trade coin) and the guilds (craftsmen who signed
their work).

## Audience & scene

and counterparties doing due diligence before awarding a contract, leasing an
asset, or engaging a service. They open the site on a phone or office laptop,
often mid-negotiation, asking: is this company real, registered, capable, and
reachable? Secondary: founders pitching Guilders Ventures.
